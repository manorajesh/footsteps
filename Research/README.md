# Research

F-4 Sensing Research prototypes (§5.1). Each camera × tracker candidate gets its own
subdirectory. Prototypes are throwaway and are not part of the Swift package.

Prototypes must still honour INV-3: no camera frames, depth maps, or skeletons are
written to disk unless explicitly opt-in for a measurement, and never committed.

The outcome is a decision record in `docs/decisions/` and a covenant amendment.
